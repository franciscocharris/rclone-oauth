Privacy Policy

Google Drive OAuth client — personal use only

Short version: this app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. It only reads and writes files inside your own Google Drive.

1. Who operates this app

This is a private Google Cloud OAuth client operated by a single individual, Francisco Charris, for personal use on their own computer. It is not offered as a service and is not used by anyone else.

2. What the app is

The app is rclone, an open-source command-line program (rclone.org). It runs locally on the operator's machine. It is used to perform encrypted backups, using restic, of the operator's own development files to the operator's own personal Google Drive account.

3. Data accessed

The OAuth token grants read and write access to Google Drive. In practice it is used exclusively to:

  • create the folders that hold the backup repository, and
  • read from and write encrypted backup chunks to those folders.

All backup data is encrypted client-side before upload. The encryption key never leaves the operator's machine and is not held by Google or by this app.

4. Data NOT collected

The app does not:

  • collect personal information of any kind,
  • run any server, endpoint, or analytics service,
  • transmit any data to the operator or to any third party,
  • access files outside the folders it created,
  • share any file with any other person or account.

There are no cookies, no tracking, no telemetry and no advertising.

5. Third parties

The only third party involved is Google, which operates Google Drive. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. No other third party receives any data.

6. Data location and retention

All data remains in the Google Drive account of the person who authorised the app. The operator is the sole owner of that account and the sole party with access to that data. Deleting the backup files, or revoking the token, removes the app's access.

7. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately and permanently removes this app's ability to read or write any Drive data. The client itself can be deleted from the Google Cloud Console.

8. Children

This app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated if the app's behaviour changes. The revision history is tracked in the public repository backing this site.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the public repository that hosts this page.
